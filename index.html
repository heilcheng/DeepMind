Projects

OpenEvals

LLM Benchmarking

Open-source evaluation framework for large language models. Runs models through standard benchmarks including MMLU, GSM8K, MATH, HumanEval, ARC, and TruthfulQA. Compares performance across model families and measures computational efficiency.

github.com/heilcheng/openevals

MedExplain Evals

Healthcare AI

Domain-specific evaluation framework for assessing how well language models explain medical information to patients. Measures accuracy, clarity, and safety of health-related responses for patient-facing applications.

github.com/heilcheng/medexplain-evals
