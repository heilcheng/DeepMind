Projects

OpenEvals

An open-source evaluation framework for large language models. Runs models through standard benchmarks including MMLU, GSM8K, MATH, HumanEval, ARC, and TruthfulQA. Compares performance across model families and measures computational efficiency.

Evaluating language models is fragmented across the field. OpenEvals provides a unified framework for consistent, reproducible benchmarks.

github.com/heilcheng/openevals
Unified LLM Benchmarking

MedExplain Evals

A domain-specific evaluation framework for assessing how well language models explain medical information to patients. Measures accuracy, clarity, and safety of health-related responses.

General benchmarks don't capture how well models communicate medical information. This matters because health misinformation can cause real harm.

github.com/heilcheng/medexplain-evals
Healthcare AI Safety
